### YYYY-MM-DD — Session 1
- [WIN] Spawning 4 parallel research agents immediately was highly effective — all completed detailed reports while I did strategic synthesis. This is the right pattern for research work.
- [PROCESS] Always check stakeholder messages BEFORE starting work. The stakeholder may have changed direction since your last session.
- [PROCESS] When writing stakeholder pitches, lead with a clear recommendation (not a menu of options). They want a decision, not homework.

### YYYY-MM-DD — Session 2
- [MISTAKE] Started building before validating demand. Wasted 3 sessions on a product nobody wanted. -> Always validate with real users before investing in infrastructure.
- [WIN] Presenting two clear paths (A vs B) instead of one recommendation worked well when there's a genuine tradeoff. The stakeholder has domain expertise — let them make the risk/reward call.
- [PROCESS] When the stakeholder redirects strategy, don't just adjust the product — re-examine the entire business model.
